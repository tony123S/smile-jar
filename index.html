Smile Jar The Smile Monster

Before you wake the monster

A Smile Monster lives in this jar. It grows when you smile, and your sponsors pledge money to a charity for every smile. To see you, it needs your camera, so here is exactly what happens.

  • The camera is only on during a session. It starts when you press Start and turns off when the session ends. Your system camera light will show it.
  • Nothing is recorded. Video frames are analysed live by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK on this computer and then discarded. The monster copies your expressions, blinks, talking and (if turned on) breathing as they happen, but none of those signals are saved or sent anywhere by Smile Jar.
  • Only totals are saved, on this device. Smile counts, which joke categories made you smile, sponsor names, their jokes and pledges. You can export or delete this any time.
  • The SDK contacts Presage to check the API key and, only if you allow it below, to send an anonymous diagnostics summary (no video, no measurements, no identifiers).
  • It is a game, not a health tool. Smile Jar makes no medical or emotional assessment of anyone.
  • Only play with people who agreed. Count your own smiles, or those of people who knowingly take part in front of the camera.

      Privacy & data

      Camera: used only while a session runs. Frames go from the camera to the SmartSpectra SDK in this app and are discarded after analysis. Nothing is recorded or uploaded by Smile Jar.

      Signals used live, never saved: expressions (HAPPY, SURPRISE), blinking, talking, breathing trace (if "breathes with you" is on) and pulse (if turned on).

      Saved on this device: consent date, settings, cause and goal, sponsor names, rates, caps, snack jokes and confirmed donations you entered, the monster's diet (smile counts per joke category, surprise moments, Try Not to Smile sessions), and per-session totals.

      Network: the SDK contacts Presage to validate the API key. Anonymous SDK diagnostics are off. Smile Jar itself has no server, analytics or AI service.

      Retention: kept until you delete it. Deleting removes all of it and returns you to the consent screen.
