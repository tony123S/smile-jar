Smile Monster

Before you meet Momo

In this game your face is the controller: Momo jumps when you smile, does magic when you look surprised, ducks when you blink and sneaks when you keep a straight face. Your sponsors also pledge money to a charity for every real smile. To see your face, the game needs your camera, so here is exactly what happens.

  • The camera is only on during a game. It starts when you press PLAY and turns off when the game ends. It is never on during the story or on the home screen. Your system camera light will show it.
  • Nothing is recorded. Video frames are analysed live by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK on this computer and then discarded. Your expressions, blinks, talking and (if turned on) breathing control Momo as they happen, but none of those signals are saved or sent anywhere by Smile Monster.
  • Only totals are saved, on this device. Smile counts, which levels you finished and your best stars and points, sponsor names, their jokes and pledges. You can export or delete this any time.
  • The SDK contacts Presage to check the API key and, only if you allow it below, to send an anonymous diagnostics summary (no video, no measurements, no identifiers).
  • It is a game, not a health tool. Smile Monster makes no medical or emotional assessment of anyone.
  • Only play with people who agreed. Count your own smiles, or those of people who knowingly take part in front of the camera.

How to play

  1. 😊SmileMomo jumps over things.
  2. 😲Look surprisedMomo zaps walls and bubbles with magic.
  3. 👉BlinkMomo ducks snowballs and pots. (If your camera can’t see blinks, a surprised face works instead.)
  4. 😐Straight faceMomo tiptoes past sleeping guards. About a second is enough.

Watch the big sign at the top: it tells you which face to make. Missed it? Momo just tumbles and you try again. Then relax your face before the next one.

❤ Bonus: every real smile also counts for charity. Your sponsors promise money for each one. You never pay anything.

        Privacy & data

        Camera: used only while a game runs, never during the story or on the home screen. Frames go from the camera to the SmartSpectra SDK in this app and are discarded after analysis. Nothing is recorded or uploaded by Smile Monster.

        Signals used live, never saved: expressions (HAPPY, SURPRISE, NEUTRAL), blinking, talking, breathing trace (if "breathes with you" is on) and pulse (if turned on).

        Saved on this device: whether you have seen the story, consent date, settings, cause and goal, sponsor names, rates, caps, snack jokes and confirmed donations you entered, adventure progress (levels finished, best stars and points), smile totals, and per-game totals.

        Momo's voice: spoken by the speech voices installed on your computer. Only on-device voices are used, so Momo's lines are never sent anywhere. Turn it off any time with the Voice button.

        Network: the SDK contacts Presage to validate the API key. Anonymous SDK diagnostics are off. Smile Monster itself has no server, analytics or AI service, and its music and art are made inside the app.

        Retention: kept until you delete it. Deleting removes all of it and starts over from the story.
