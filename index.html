Smile Jar

Before we open the jar

Smile Jar turns your smiles into pledges for a cause. To count smiles it needs your camera, so here is exactly what happens.

  • The camera is only on during a Smile Session. It starts when you press Start and turns off when the session ends. Your system camera light will show it.
  • Nothing is recorded. Video frames are analysed live by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK on this computer and then discarded. No video, photos, face data or expression scores are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Only totals are saved, on this device. For each session we keep the date, length and number of smiles, so the jar can add up pledges. You can export or delete this any time.
  • The SDK contacts Presage to check the API key and, only if you allow it below, to send an anonymous diagnostics summary (no video, no measurements, no identifiers).
  • It is a game, not a health tool. Smile Jar makes no medical or emotional assessment of anyone.
  • Only count your own smiles, or those of people who have agreed to take part and are in front of the camera knowingly.

    Privacy & data

    Camera: used only while a Smile Session runs. Frames go from the camera to the SmartSpectra SDK in this app and are discarded after analysis. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

    Saved on this device: your consent date, settings, cause, sponsor names and rates, and per-session totals (date, length, smile count, pledged amount). Nothing else.

    Network: the SDK contacts Presage to validate the API key. Anonymous SDK diagnostics are off. Smile Jar itself has no server, analytics or AI service.

    Retention: kept until you delete it. Deleting removes all of it and returns you to the consent screen.
